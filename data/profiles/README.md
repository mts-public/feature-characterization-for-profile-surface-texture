Case studies taken over from publication: 
"Crossing-The-Line Segmentation as a Basis for Rsm and Rc Evaluation"

- `*.smd`: original softgauge files according to ISO 5436-2 incl. the step
  size dx (read with `smd2mat` in MATLAB or `read_smd` in Python)
- `*.mat`: profile values z in µm (identical to the z-values of the `*.smd`
  files)
